What are Amino Acids?

Amino acids are organic molecules that serve as the building blocks of proteins. Each standard amino acid contains an amino group, a carboxyl group, a hydrogen atom and a variable side chain attached to a central alpha carbon.

Why are Amino Acids Important?

🧬

Protein Formation

Amino acids join through peptide bonds to form proteins and polypeptide chains.

⚙️

Enzyme Activity

The amino acid sequence helps determine protein structure and enzyme function.

🔬

Bioinformatics

Amino acid sequences are studied to understand protein structure, function and evolution.
